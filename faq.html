Common Questions

Frequently asked questions about appointments, services, and care at Sugar Lakes Family Practice.

Last Updated: November 2026
Are you currently accepting new patients in Sugar Land or Rosenberg?
Yes. I am currently accepting new patients at Sugar Lakes Family Practice in Sugar Land, TX, serving families across Fort Bend County including Rosenberg and Richmond. To schedule an appointment, please call our office at (281) 565-2800.
What services do you provide?
I provide comprehensive primary care for the whole family (children and adults). This includes chronic disease management (such as diabetes and hypertension), preventive screenings, annual wellness exams, and acute care for minor illnesses.
Do you provide Telemedicine or Virtual Visits?
Yes. We offer secure telemedicine options for appropriate follow-up visits and lab reviews to provide convenience for patients in the Greater Houston area. Please call the office at (281) 565-2800 to confirm whether your visit is eligible for telemedicine.
Do you perform Sports Physicals?
Yes. I offer UIL-compliant Pre-Participation Physical Evaluations for student athletes in Lamar CISD, Fort Bend ISD, and surrounding school districts.
What is a "Medical Home"?
A "Medical Home" is a model of care where your primary physician leads a team that cares for you. It means having a doctor who knows your history, coordinates your specialist care, and helps you navigate the healthcare system, rather than just treating you when you are sick.
Do you provide Obstetrics (OB) care?
No. While I provide general women's health and gynecological care, I do not practice obstetrics or deliver babies.
What insurance plans do you accept?
Sugar Lakes Family Practice accepts most major insurance plans. Because coverage can vary by specific policy, please call our office at (281) 565-2800 or visit sugarlakesfamilypractice.com to verify your plan before scheduling.
