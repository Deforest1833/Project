Biography

Clinical Excellence & Compassion

Dr. Safwan Moton, DO is a Board-Certified Family Medicine Physician practicing at Sugar Lakes Family Practice in Sugar Land, Texas. He brings a strong foundation in evidence-based medicine and a commitment to comprehensive primary care for patients across Fort Bend County.

Dr. Moton combines rigorous medical science with real-world clinical experience. He provides care rooted in established guidelines and tailored to effective, long-term outcomes. This commitment to clinical excellence earned him recognition as a "Top 10% Most Cited Clinician" by Doximity.

Philosophy

Patient-Centered Approach

Dr. Moton believes that informed patients make healthier choices. He takes the time to clearly explain diagnoses and treatment options, utilizing modern tools to ensure you feel confident about your healthcare journey.
